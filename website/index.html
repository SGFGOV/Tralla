Nearby People Detection

Find and connect with people in your vicinity using passive technology without requiring explicit permissions.

Real-time Chat

Start text or voice conversations with nearby people and stay connected even when you're apart.

Group Creation

Create groups with people you meet for shared interests, events, or activities and stay organized.

Expense Splitting

Group Activities

How Tralla Works

1

Download the App

Get the Tralla app from the App Store or Google Play Store and set up your profile.

2

Discover Nearby Users

Tralla uses passive technology to help you discover users in your vicinity without requiring explicit permissions.

3

Connect and Chat

Connect with people nearby, chat in real-time, and build your social network based on your actual surroundings.

4

Create Groups and Activities

Form groups with people you meet, organize activities, split expenses, and enjoy seamless social interactions.
